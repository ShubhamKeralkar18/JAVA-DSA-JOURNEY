## Q3 - Factorial

Factorial of n is:

n! = n × (n-1) × ... × 1

Example:

5! = 5 × 4 × 3 × 2 × 1
5! = 120

A for loop is used inside the method.

## Q4 - Prime Number

A prime number has exactly two factors:

1 and itself.

Examples:

2, 3, 5, 7, 11

The isPrime() method checks whether the number is divisible by any number from 2 to n-1.

It returns:

true → Prime
false → Not Prime
